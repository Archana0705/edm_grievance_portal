Help & FAQ

The TN eSevai Grievance Portal is a two-way communication platform that enables citizens to register their grievances related to the delivery of TN eSevai services by TNeGA and have them resolved conveniently in one place. They can:

  • File their grievance(s) anytime and from anywhere.
  • Track their grievance(s) and see the status.

Follow these steps:

  • Sign up on the portal with your mobile number/email ID. Create user credentials if you are using your email ID.
  • Log in to the portal with your mobile number, OTP, or user credentials.
  • Click "File a Grievance".
  • Describe your grievance and fill in the form to the best of your knowledge.
  • Click "Submit".
  • Your grievance will be registered with the concerned department(s), and you will receive status updates via SMS.

Go to this website: https://tngis.tnega.org/edm-grievance-portal/index.html

And follow these steps:

  • If you are reporting a grievance for the first time, click “New User? Register here” and enter your mobile number.
  • Otherwise, enter your mobile number in the Citizen Login section.
  • Enter the OTP you receive via SMS and click “Verify OTP”.
  • On the Menu tab, click Report Issue.
  • Select the relevant Type, Category, and Sub-category from the dropdown menu.
  • Enter all required information, upload supporting documents, and click Submit.

You can try one of the following alternatives:

  • Visit an e-Sevai centre nearest to you and report your grievance.
  • Call the helpline – Dial 1100.

You can submit a grievance in Tamil or English.

You can submit grievances of the following types:

  • e-Sevai centre related
  • e-Sevai officials related
  • e-Sevai portals and services

You will find relevant categories and sub-categories listed under each of these types once you select them from the drop-down menu. If your grievance does not fall under any of the listed categories, you can choose the Other option and describe your grievance in detail.
