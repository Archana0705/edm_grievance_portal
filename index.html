About E-Sevai Grievance Portal

The EDM Grievance Redressal Portal is an official platform empowering citizens, streamlining service delivery, and enhancing accountability across government departments.

  • Citizen-Centric
    Easy registration and real-time tracking.
  • Operator Network
    Support at eSevai centres.
  • Department Accountability
    Clear workflows and ownership.
  • Official Monitoring
    Analytics for TNeGA oversight.
