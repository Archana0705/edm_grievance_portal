About Us

About the E-Sevai Grievance Portal

The EDM Grievance Redressal Portal is a centralized platform designed to empower citizens, streamline operations, and enhance accountability across all levels of governance.

  • Citizen-Centric: Easy grievance registration and real-time tracking.
  • Efficient Operators: Seamless support through eSevai operators and helplines.
  • Department Accountability: Clear workflows and defined responsibility channels.
  • Data-Driven Monitoring: Insights for eDMs & TNeGA officials.
“Complaints and their resolution are fundamental to democracy — ensuring justice and empowerment for every citizen.”
